## Information architecture
- The main nav strip reads **Home · Movies · Series · Live TV · Library · Search**. TVM wordmark top-left, profile orb top-right.
- The **profile orb** is the single top-level account action. OK on it opens the *You* menu, a glass side panel listing TVM Hub, Switch profile, Settings, Account & Plan and Sources (studio hubs / Real-Debrid / Live sources). Everything the old 17-button ribbon reached stays reachable through the strip or this menu.
- **TVM Hub** tabs are Network (default) · Devices · Activity. The player's Control Centre gets a Devices tab later, opening the same inspector.
- Back order: modal → inspector → content → tab strip → previous route. Focus is restored from per-route and per-tab keys built from **ids, never indexes**.

## Design tokens: the 10-foot scale
- **Canvas:** Roku keeps the 3840×2160 canvas (scale 0.5 at FHD, 0.333 at HD). The tokens below are given in **1080p reference px**, so Roku uses ×2 canvas units.
- **Safe insets:** 80 px left/right and 60 px top/bottom. Full-bleed artwork may extend past them.
- **Type** (Inter):

  | Role | Size | Weight |
  |---|---|---|
  | Hero title | 72 | Bold |
  | Screen title | 48 | Bold |
  | Primary label | 30 | Semibold, or Bold where Semibold isn't available |
  | Secondary | 24 | Regular |
  | Caption / meta | 22 | Regular |

  Nothing is smaller than 20 px at 1080p. At 720p every size must stay ≥ 18 device px, so where Roku HD needs it, scale up by 1.15 at HD.
- **Focus:** one target at a time. It gets scale 1.04 (posters) or 1.06 (pills and nodes), an edge highlight that works on light and dark art, a 6 px lift and a 150 ms ease-out.
- **Panel moves:** 220 ms. Tab highlight slides take 180 ms. Every animation can be interrupted, and logical focus updates before the animation starts.
- States are visually distinct: **focused** (rim plus scale), **active tab** (filled pill, no rim), **selected device** (soft fill plus marker, shown while focus is in the inspector), **pressed** (scale 0.98 for 80 ms) and **disabled** (40% opacity plus a "not available" label).
- **Wrapping:** rails and lists do NOT wrap. Tabs do not wrap. Edges are hard stops.

## Glass on Roku

- Materials use standard SceneGraph Posters and checked-in PNGs generated by `apps/roku/scripts/make-glass.mjs`. Signed-distance silhouettes are integrated at 4 ? 4 samples per output pixel. Assets are authored in 3840-canvas units, twice the 1080p reference size. Every 9-patch has black stretch/content guides and transparent guide corners.
- One top-left light source drives the shared layer stack, from back to front.
  1. A soft black 33% drop shadow, offset 12 canvas units downward with an approximately 36-unit blur footprint.
  2. An antialiased rounded white fill 9-patch, tinted with `blendColor` at about 12% opacity on dark scenes. Rounded fills cover the complete silhouette.
  3. A white vertical body gradient, 16% at the top to 3% at the bottom.
  4. A curved elliptical gloss over the upper 45%, fading from 26% to 2%, with a 2?4 canvas-unit softened lower edge.
  5. A 3-unit directional rim, approximately 75% white toward the upper left to 15% toward the lower right.
  6. A 2-unit inner dark line at 18% opacity.
  7. An 8% bottom inner reflection.
- Panel, pill, tab, chip and poster-card silhouettes share these layers. Separate sizes keep small tabs, chips and progress tracks within their 9-patch cap dimensions. Redesigned surfaces use rounded fill PNGs because Rectangle cornerRadius renders square in the emulator and on older firmware.
- Focus raises the rim toward 95%, adds a separate approximately 24-unit / 30% outer-glow 9-patch, strengthens the shadow and uses the 1.06 pill/node scale with a 12-unit lift. Poster cards use 1.04. Active navigation and tabs have an 85% frosted white fill and dark ink. Selected devices retain a soft blue-white rim. Press scales to 0.98 for 80 ms. Disabled controls retain 40% opacity.
- Hub devices are 392-unit glass spheres, with a 420-unit warm-silver account sphere. They share an upper-centre radial body, upper-left elliptical specular, edge Fresnel light, a bottom-right crescent and a faint lower-plane reflection. Focus adds a warm-white halo. The larger star keeps stable device ids/slots and a six-device limit, with space between the expanded nodes. Its translucent ellipse has about 5% fill and a brighter far rim. Spokes have a luminous 6-unit core and a soft falloff.
- Ambient surfaces decode the current successfully loaded trending hero at only 96 ? 54 and stretch it under a dark scrim. Settings has one Background choice under Appearance, defaulting to Trending, with Lighthouse as the fixed ambient alternative. The permanent missing-art Home fallback is a 1280 ? 720 public-domain NOAA photograph, sourced and licensed in `apps/roku/images/ASSETS.md`. Catalogue art remains full-bleed on Home. A 300 ms debounce preloads the incoming A/B hero before its crossfade, then releases the hidden full-size texture. Copy and playback targets follow the successfully displayed title.
- Reduce Transparency uses opaque ink fills and contrast-safe rims. Reduce Motion removes focus scale, lift and sliding while keeping state indicators and focus order. Both remain registry-backed Settings controls. Materials have no repeating animation.
- Budgets remain about 1,500 live nodes on Home, 400 ? 225 landscape / 300 ? 450 portrait artwork decodes, a 1920 ? 1080 maximum hero decode, and a packaged zip below 4 MB. Inter Regular, SemiBold and Bold ship with their OFL licence. SemiBold's official release-tag source and SHA-256 are pinned in `sync-assets.mjs`.

## Contracts
- Hub: `docs/redesign/HUB-V1-CONTRACT.md`.
- Pricing: base TVM is 1000/9000 pence (month/year) and Live TV is an add-on at 700/6000. Both come from the server price book (`GET /api/pricing/book`, `POST /api/pricing/quote`). These are not under `/api/billing`, because that prefix is loopback-only and TVs on the LAN need quotes. Clients never hard-code prices. Live TV defaults to visible as "Coming soon" and is **not orderable** until the owner flips the flag.
